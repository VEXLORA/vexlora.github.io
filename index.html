Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before working with Vexlora.

What types of security assessments do you offer?

We provide black‑box, grey‑box, and white‑box penetration testing, vulnerability scanning, source code review, and cloud configuration audits across AWS, Azure, and GCP.

How long does a typical security audit take?

Depending on scope, most audits complete within 5–15 business days. We deliver a detailed report with severity ratings, proof‑of‑concept, and prioritized remediation steps.

Do you build websites from scratch?

Yes. We design and develop high‑security web platforms from the ground up, using modern stacks and security‑first architecture, or we can harden your existing site.

Do you offer ongoing monitoring and support?

Absolutely. We offer monthly retainers with 24/7 monitoring, incident response, threat intelligence, and continuous vulnerability management.
